## Architectural Principles

- Build from the bottom upward.
- The architecture is a destination, not a fixed implementation.
- Future components should not be implemented before they are needed.
- Keep components testable and understandable.
- Let engineering discoveries influence the architecture.
- Human decision-making remains part of the system.
- The system supports human decision-making rather than replacing it.
